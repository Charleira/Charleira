👋 About Me
I'm a Computer Engineering student (graduating 2027) passionate about building intelligent systems that turn data into decisions. My journey started in back-end and full-stack development, and today I work at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, and Business Intelligence.
🤖 Currently developing an AI agent that forecasts stock prices on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) — my undergraduate thesis, now being extended into a research paper.
📊 Working as a Data & BI Analyst, building Power BI dashboards, DAX models, and automated data pipelines over large corporate datasets.
🎓 Preparing to pursue a Master's degree in Artificial Intelligence & Applied AI abroad.
🌱 Always learning — currently deepening Machine Learning, Go, and improving my English (IELTS/TOEFL prep).
